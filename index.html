Nota: a free online notepad that works offline

Write notes right in your browser. No account, no ads and no sign-up. Your notes stay on your device and keep working without internet.

What you can do

  • Format text with headings, bold, italic, underline, highlight, links, to-do lists, quotes and code.
  • Find and replace inside a note.
  • Keep many notes, sort them into folders and pin the important ones.
  • Save any note as Markdown, plain text or PDF.
  • Pick a font, a text size, and a light or dark theme.
  • Back up all your notes to one file and restore them later.

Private by design

Notes are saved in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. If you clear your browser data your notes are removed, so download a backup from the File menu now and then.
